Lasagna

One of the most recognisable dishes from Italian cuisine. Learn how to make your very own Lasagna following this recipe.

Ingredients

  • Pasta Sfoglia
  • Ragù Sauce
  • Mozzarella
  • Parmigiano Grattugiato
  • Any extras you'd like to use!

Steps

  1. Put the first layer of Pasta Sfoglia in a tray
  2. Put a layer of Ragù sauce on top of the Pasta Sfoglia
  3. Scatter some small cubes of Mozzarella over the sauce
  4. If you have any extras for your Lasagna add them now
  5. Repeat the previous steps (1-4) until your tray is full or you run out of those ingredients
  6. Sprinkle a lot of Parmigiano Grattugiato on top of the last layer
  7. Put the tray in the pre-heated oven at 180°C for 30 minutes
  8. Enjoy your Lasagna!
