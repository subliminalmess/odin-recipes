Omelette

A classic of the French cuisine. You don't need many ingredients and it will make sure you feel full for a long time.

Ingredients

  • 3 Eggs per person
  • Grated Cheese
  • Salt and Pepper

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs open and put the yolk and white into a bowl. Throw away the shell
  2. Make sure there are no shell pieces in the bowl. Remove them if they sneaked in
  3. Take a fork and start mixing vigorously until you obtain a yellow cream
  4. Add some grated cheese on top of the cream and mix again with the fork
  5. Put a pan on the stove and wait until it becomes hot enough
  6. If necessary, mix the eggs and cheese once more to obtain a smooth consistency
  7. Pour the cream in the pan and let it cook on slow heat for 5 minutes
  8. When the underside of your omelette is cooked flip it. You may use a plate or lid to help yourself in the task
  9. Let the other side cook for another 3-5 minutes
  10. Add Salt and Pepper to your liking
  11. Serve and enjoy!
